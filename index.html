🐰 Rabbit Games 🌸

Two small games about a rabbit, a gardener, and a hidden probability distribution.

🌿💣

Bard the Gardener

Play as Bard. A rabbit is hiding somewhere along the garden path, and you get to lob bombs at it until you find it. The rabbit's hiding spot is drawn from a hidden distribution — can you learn where it likes to hide?
